                  Company holidays

                  The business is closed. Each person's working days in the closure come out of their allowance, and staff get an update. Add bank holidays here too if you close on them.

                    Work locations

                    Staff can only clock in or out within this distance of a work location. Phone locations can be out by a few metres, so up to 50 m extra is allowed for a weak signal. To pin a location exactly, stand inside it and tap "Set to where I am now".

                      Contracts and overtime rates

                      Each person signs in to the app with their own PIN (4 to 8 numbers; 6 to 8 for Jackie, whose PIN also opens this planner). Tell them theirs in person. Anyone without a PIN can't sign in. Hours worked above the contracted hours in a week count as overtime, paid at the same hourly rate. Monthly pay is worked out the bookkeepers' way: weekly hours × 52 ÷ 12. Leave working days blank if they vary. Changes save straight away.

                      Allowances

                      Holiday days per year. Change an entitlement and it saves straight away. Allowances reset on 1 January.
